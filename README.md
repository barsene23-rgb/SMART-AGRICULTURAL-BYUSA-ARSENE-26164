# SMART AGRICULTURAL SUPPLY CHAIN & MARKET INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
## Scope Definition

### **Business Overview**

This integrated agricultural platform connects farmers and buyers while automating harvest registration, pricing intelligence, order matching, logistics allocation, and spoilage monitoring. Real-time data on crop quality, storage conditions, and market demand enables smart recommendations, reduces waste, and ensures faster movement of produce from farm to market.

By creating transparent digital transactions and farmer financial profiles, the system improves revenue access, supports loan eligibility, and eliminates unnecessary intermediaries. It functions as a full agricultural MIS, strengthening market forecasting, supply chain efficiency, and national food security through reliable, data-driven decision-making.

### **Problem Statement**

Farmers in Rwanda lack real-time market intelligence and structured buyer access, which results in dependence on intermediaries and significant income loss. Weak logistics coordination and poor storage monitoring contribute to high post-harvest spoilage rates, reducing national food supply efficiency. Additionally, the absence of digital financial records prevents farmers from accessing credit, limiting growth and long-term agricultural sustainability.

### **Key Objectives**

***1. System Objectives***

* Automate the full agricultural supply chain, including harvest registration, real-time pricing, matching, and logistics coordination.

* Reduce post-harvest losses through continuous spoilage monitoring and timely reallocation alerts.

* Increase farmer revenue by enabling direct market access and eliminating exploitative intermediaries.

***2. Management Objectives***

* Strengthen national food security by improving harvest movement efficiency and storage utilization.

* Enhance transparency in pricing, transactions, and product flow across the supply chain.

* Enable farmer financial inclusion by generating digital transaction histories and credit eligibility profiles.

***3. Technical Objectives***

* Implement advanced PL/SQL predictive analytics for pricing, spoilage detection, and demand forecasting.

* Maintain high system performance with fast response times, efficient data processing, and scalable architecture.

* Integrate automated triggers, schedulers, and reporting tools for continuous real-time intelligence and decision support.

### **Quick Start Instructions**

***Register Core Data***

Insert farmers, crops, buyers, storage facilities, and initial market prices into their respective tables.

***Record Harvests***

Use the harvest registration procedure to input quantity, quality grade, and storage details.

***Run Matching & Transactions***

Execute the MATCHING_ENGINE package to pair farmers with buyers, then process orders through the TRANSACTIONS table.

***Monitor Spoilage & Prices***

Start DBMS_SCHEDULER jobs for spoilage alerts and price tracking, triggering notifications when risk or price changes occur.

***Generate Reports***

Use REPORT_GENERATOR to view price trends, storage usage, spoilage predictions, and credit scoring summaries.
