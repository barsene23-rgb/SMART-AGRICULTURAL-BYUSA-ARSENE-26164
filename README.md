# SMART AGRICULTURAL SUPPLY CHAIN & MARKET INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
## Scope Definition

**Business Overview**

This integrated agricultural platform connects farmers and buyers while automating harvest registration, pricing intelligence, order matching, logistics allocation, and spoilage monitoring. Real-time data on crop quality, storage conditions, and market demand enables smart recommendations, reduces waste, and ensures faster movement of produce from farm to market.

By creating transparent digital transactions and farmer financial profiles, the system improves revenue access, supports loan eligibility, and eliminates unnecessary intermediaries. It functions as a full agricultural MIS, strengthening market forecasting, supply chain efficiency, and national food security through reliable, data-driven decision-making.
